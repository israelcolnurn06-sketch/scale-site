Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Iscale Solutions ("Iscale," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form and booking details: name, email address, and anything you write in the "what would you like to discuss" field when you request a strategy call.
  • Usage data: standard analytics information such as pages visited, device type, and referring source, collected through analytics tools if and when they are enabled on this site.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a call.
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • To send you information you've asked for. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Third-party tools

We may use standard analytics and advertising platforms to understand traffic and measure ad performance. These tools may set cookies in your browser. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention

We keep contact information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to your inquiry or maintain our business records, and delete it on request where we're not required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing iscalemarketing@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: iscalemarketing@gmail.com.
